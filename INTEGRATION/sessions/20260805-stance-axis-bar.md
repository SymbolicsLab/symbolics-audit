## 1. What was on trial

`tension` is defined as the non-acceptance signature on an **objectified** relation, so the inhabited/objectified
**stance axis** carried two jobs:

- **Job A — the category boundary:** proto-tension (the pre-objectified clash, present from L1) vs tension proper.
- **Job B — the shimmer-orthogonality:** the result that cleared bar 2 (non-redundancy against the vault's
  closest existing category).

Report v2 §8 called the axis "the single articulation that does not simply re-describe committed structure" — i.e.
suspected NEW, hence a bar under the entailment-vs-assemblage discipline. Phase D fixed the **grade** and touched
neither job, so `03_Phenomenology §Tension` could not be written: without stance you can state neither the
category boundary nor the orthogonality.

Two candidate homes stood pre-named, both **cross-level** while the axis was wanted **in-level at L3** — billed as
the work:

1. operating-under vs representing-as (the ascension mechanism);
2. apparatus-opacity vs de-naturalization — carrying a testable and possibly false consequence (you could hold
   tension only about relations you have de-naturalized, and people appear to feel torn about things they have not).

**Neither route was taken.**

## 2. The run

### Job A — absorbed by the 08-04 relocation (the flag predicted this)

Under the relocation, proto-tension is pressure at a boundary with **no rival placement yet formulated**; tension
proper is **two candidates on the table**. No separate act of objectification is required to get from one to the
other, and the reason is PB's own Occam move: **the relation between two placements is itself a distinction**,
which is why nothing was added when "seam" retired. Having tension proper therefore *just is* having drawn that
second-order distinction — and a distinction whose relata are two placements of a cut cannot be drawn without
having that cut available as something that could be drawn otherwise, which is the whole content of "objectified."

**Objectification is entailed by the rivalry condition, not added to it.**

Side gain: this answers **Frege's criterion demand** better than the axis did. He asked for a criterion
distinguishing *with certainty in any given case*. *Is a rival formulated · does it deliver differently on a shared
case · are there stakes* is checkable case-by-case; an inhabited/objectified stance is introspective, and
introspective criteria are what his demand was aimed at.

### Job B — re-cleared on better ground; the 08-04 flag was wrong here

The flag said Job B was untouched and would still need the axis. It does not.

*The difficulty expected:* shimmer's first aspect is oscillation at the fold with "both readings live," which after
the relocation sounds like two placements — so the relocation looked to worsen the redundancy risk.

*Why it does not:* the vault's paradigm case of shimmer is the **Rubin vase** (`01_Ontology §Entanglement`:
figure/ground, "both poles live, neither permanently backgrounded") — the two **poles of one cut**, which is the
exact structure PB's *dog vs cat* counterexample rules OUT of tension. Both driven, nothing foreclosed, no cost.
**The discriminating case is vault-anchored and falls out of PB's own refutation.**

All four combinations populate with no stance anywhere:

| | no tension | tension |
|---|---|---|
| **no shimmer** | ordinary settled cut, no rival | the practitioner who has known for years her category mishandles a class of cases — both placements formulated, real stakes, no flicker |
| **shimmer** | the Rubin vase; any fold still settling with no rival and nothing riding on it | a live re-carving under stakes |

**The orthogonality is carried by an object-difference, not by two axes:** shimmer is the felt register of **one
fold's** non-settledness and remainder-trace; tension is the cost of a formulated rivalry between **two
placements**. Independent, because a fold can be unsettled with no rival formulated and rock-settled with a rival
formulated and expensive.

⟢ **This is why the relocation helps rather than hurts.** Before it, tension was between the two sides of *one*
cut — structurally almost on top of shimmer, also about one fold — and that near-collision is what forced a stance
axis in to hold them apart. Two placements versus one fold, and non-redundancy is free.

### The acceptance axis is stale too

The grid says **tension present ⟺ non-acceptance**. Phase D says the grade is fixed by stakes on the far side plus
how much actualizing this side forecloses; acceptance is not among the inputs. The 08-02/03 adjudication makes the
collision explicit in its own words: *declining the election refuses the hardening, not the bill — the remainder
presses whether or not anyone has hardened the seam.*

**If the bill is paid whether or not you accept, acceptance does not determine whether tension is present.** Grid
cell 1 (relation held as object, accepted, folding-into-contradiction seen as optional) still carries cost wherever
the stakes are. So bar 2's clearance **as originally stated** fails with the right-hand biconditional, and is
re-established on the object-difference instead.

**The grid is superseded in both axes.**

## 3. ★★ PB's diagnosis — the genetic explanation (PB, 2026-08-05)

The structural finding above shows only that the axis is in the wrong place. It does not explain why an axis was
put there. PB's move does.

**The first fold PB had in mind when the grid was built was the "+" of L5+ — the plastic act.** His original
understanding: *holding mutually incompatible frames about oneself until one sees "through" them, acknowledging
that they both apply* — that acknowledgment being the "accepting" — **and this is where shimmer is felt strongest,
because *I* is the thing being folded.**

In that case, formulating the rival genuinely *feels* like an acceptance, because what is acknowledged is that a
rival carving of **you** has purchase.

> **So "accepting" was never a stance taken toward a relation. It was the RIVALRY CONDITION encountered at the
> self-applicative fold, then generalized to L3 — where the self-flavour drops away and the description stops
> fitting.**

⟢ This is the study running **its own level-confusion diagnosis on itself**: a structure read off one level and
taken for the whole. Same shape as the 08-04 finding that classical logic formalized L3 and took it for the whole
of reasoning.

### PB's structural proposal

**`me + remainder` (or `me − intrusion`) = new type space**, because "me" is the whole stack reflexively looking
back on itself. This is the **08-04 generator result applied at L5+** — remainder pressing in proposes a wider
placement, intrusion pressing out a narrower one — with the contested cut being the self. Vocabulary is committed
(`02_Operations §Within L5+`: "a reorganization of the system's own type-space").

**Three checks, all passing:**

1. **The rivalry condition is met in the right currency.** 08-05 requires rivals to deliver differently, with
   **non-causal** consequences. The vault says that under a type-space reorganization *"some prior expressions
   cease to be well-formed; others acquire new meaning"* — divergent **licensing**, which is exactly the 08-05
   gloss (*a placement never changes what happens; it changes what is licensed*).
2. **No level violation.** "Tension proper is L3" must be read as *the level at which rivalry first becomes
   formulable*, not as a ceiling — which is what the formal finding already says (checkpoint 6: tension is
   level-agnostic; seams need no levelhood; self-pressure presses at the self's level). Proto-tension from L1,
   rivalry from L3, the "+" as the instance where the contested cut is the self.
3. **"Shimmer strongest at the +" falls out rather than being posited.** Shimmer is the felt register of a fold's
   non-settledness; at L5+ the self-fold is *constitutively* unable to close (permanent T-failure), so a fold that
   can never settle, being re-placed, is the maximal case. The operative-column walk reached the same point from
   the other direction (compound affect tracks captured-row richness, most dramatic at the "+").

## 4. The three senses "acceptance" was conflating

The real yield of the exchange. One word carried three distinct structural positions:

- **(a) the rivalry condition** — two placements formulated, both driven. PB's sense at the "+". Belongs to the
  **object**, not to a stance.
- **(b) not-hardening** — declining the election. Survives unmoved as the **acceptance-triad** (accept → tension →
  elective contradiction); about whether you force the exclusion, not about whether you pay.
- **(c) the subsumption outcome** — the new whole that includes both, located at the "+" by PB's 07-31 settlement
  (*where both sides are genuinely load-bearing the redrawing must include both*). The **resolution** of a tension,
  not a stance during one.

**This explains the grid's internal strain:** cell 1 was reaching for (b), cell 4 for (c), and the column heading
was really (a).

  acceptable.
