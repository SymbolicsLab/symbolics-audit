## The structural result

**Both credential-open doors require a supervisor's recommendation letter** — Concordia's
direct-entry-from-bachelor's ("a recommendation letter from a research supervisor, affirming your research
experience and capabilities", flagged on their page as a difference from standard applications) and McGill's
PhD1 direct entry. **The credential problem therefore does not resolve into an admissions problem; it
resolves into the person problem, by regulation, at every door.**

`DIRECTION.md` §1 (person first, structure second) and `DISCLOSURE-MOVES.md` ("there is no institutional
front door") were derived from historical precedent and from the Manning outcome. They are now independently
re-derived from the current admission regulations of both universities. This is the strongest external
support the practice frame has received, and it means the person-first rule is no longer a strategic
preference that could be traded away for an easier structural route — there is no such route.
