> **New: electiveness plus a graded resting register whose grade is denominated in mattering.**

The reading survives untouched; the two neighbourhoods are still empty at the intersection. What sharpened is

### 3. Step 12 — injected (PB, this session)

**Item 52, the expressive debt — injected as a *test* rather than an open hole.** Report v2 §7.

The debt: if contradiction is elective, and a second body says *"you are inconsistent,"* the first can decline —
so what does the verdict *do*? Priest pays with a primitive non-content-bearing rejection; Berto says pragmatic
exclusion is too thin and a primitive content-exclusion is needed on pain of trivialism.

The candidate answer the mattering derivation supplies is **a third route**:

> **The verdict's expressive content is *here is a cost you are already paying*.**

Declining refuses the hardening, **not the bill**. The bite is at the **value** level — not content (Berto), not
act (Priest). And trivialism blocks without a primitive content-exclusion: **a body that accepts everything has
nothing at stake anywhere**, so every grade goes to zero — the stale/crystallized limit, a dead apparatus rather
than a maximally permissive one. **Trivialism is unreachable because it is costless.**

**Three tests for Phase D**, and the third is the one to watch:

1. Can two parties genuinely **disagree** in cost terms, with a fact of the matter (*what B's stack actually
   holds and drives*)? If it bottoms out in say-so, **Berto's too-thin charge lands here exactly as it lands on
   Priest.**
2. Do **cost and resilience come apart**? Predicted divergences: *high-cost/high-resilience* (the tragic case;
   the artwork held at full price for a relevant reason) and *zero-cost/wrong* (the naturalized cut nobody has a
   stake in). If they collapse, cost is resilience under another name and §3 adds nothing.
3. **Is the mattering derivation overloaded?** The same move is asked to discharge the entailment bar and pay
   the expressive debt. **The two must be separately falsifiable; if Phase D cannot fail one without failing the
   other, they have been welded and one of them is not being argued.**
