## 1. The L3 route — worked, corrected by PB, and now stronger

**The question.** The 08-04 relocation left *rivalry is constituted by consequence, and consequence is L3* as a
Claude-generated, unratified claim — load-bearing, since it is what decoupled the level-assignment from the
unratified stance axis. PB pressed it: **are we mistaking consequence for mattering/stakes?**

**The first Claude answer, WITHDRAWN.** It disambiguated three senses of "consequence" (L3 entailment ·
practical outcome · cost), showed the relocation's examples slide between them, and proposed that consequence
and stakes are two separable co-conditions — resting on a test case of *propagation without stakes* (a
taxonomist idly weighing two placements, rich downstream nomenclature, nothing riding on it).

**PB's refutation.** *Distinction and mattering co-constitute; there is no idle distinction-making.* The case is
malformed: what it describes is a nomenclature **sitting in a book** — captured-row form with no active-row
firing, i.e. **stale representation**, not a low-stakes distinction. **The fifth instance of the
register-conflation family in a week, and the first that is Claude's own.**

**PB's replacement, adopted.** *Without considering a consequence at all, on what basis could we even consider
rival boundaries?* Under co-constitution **every** live cut is driven, so drivenness cannot be the differentia
between an ordinary cut and a contested one. What differs is that **two placements** are driven — and two
placements can stand as rivals only if they **deliver differently**. Therefore **rival placements are not
formulable below L3**: unformulable, not merely cheaper. The L3 condition is not an add-on; it is already inside
the case that forced the relocation.

**⚠ The consequences must be NON-CAUSAL, and this is forced.** If rivalry were constituted by causal
consequence the contest would be field-adjudicable and contradiction would acquire the field-court standing the
study's centre denies. It costs nothing: non-causal dependence articulates level-by-level and **L3 entailment
is one of its kinds**. **Correction to the relocation's own gloss:** "umbrella or not" is field-side and should
be dropped; "claim or not" is right. ***A placement never changes what happens; it changes what is licensed.***

**Not a third ingredient** (Claude, unruled). Consequence-relations are folds, and the apparatus is one dynamic
described at structural locations, so the grade's inputs remain the two faces. **L3 is where the object is
formulable, and a level-assignment is not an ingredient.**

**The battery, PB-endorsed ("your examples settle it"), non-causal throughout:** 1-as-prime (zero causal
content — the clean case) · open-texture "vehicle" · **the attachment test, which is a PREDICTION** (an
uncontested boundary becomes contested exactly when a constitutive liability attaches to it) · the DSM
subtraction test · **the sub-L3 negative control, which is the falsifier** (threshold drift forfeits nothing,
so no contest). **Identity terms are L5 (PB)** — the reflexive licensing structure — hence not a counterexample
to an L3 claim.

**⚠ Residual, unruled.** The assignment is now close to **analytic** — true in virtue of what "rival placement"
means — which changes its status from a defended level-assignment to a consequence of the object's definition;
the substance moves downstream into the three discriminations. And the inability to construct a counterexample
is weak evidence of exactly the shape the method file's first standing guard warns about.
