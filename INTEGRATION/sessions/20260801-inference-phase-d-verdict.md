## The declaration

**CONDITIONAL discharge**, declared explicitly as the brief required.

The bar — *is the grade fixed by the two faces of the one dynamic, with nothing third smuggled in?*
— is a **compositional** question: are these two the inputs, and only these two. It is well-posed
whether or not the inputs are themselves grounded. The **absolute** reading would make the bar
unpassable while the for-structure is owed, which is the *unpassable-by-construction* failure the
TERMINUS entry just diagnosed in the Dependence court-test; and it would make the for-structure
load-bearing in a fourth place.

**The declaration is not the cheap reading.** Three routes could have failed the bar *even granting
both faces* — partition-shape, a count of episodes, field-joints/worlds. The first was live and
forced a correction to the study's own statement of its object.

## The verdict: the bar clears — but the parts sit differently than the report has them

- **Partition contributes the exclusion, and it is binary** (`01_Ontology §Distinction is
  constitutively binary`). **So the gradation cannot come from the partition face** — and the
  report's §2 object ("graded mutual-exclusivity, soft to hard") is not what §3 grounds.
- **Both graded quantities are value-face and both are vault-native:** stakes on the far side, and
  **how much of it actualizing this side forecloses** — the latter being `03_Phenomenology
  §Remainder and actualization`, already graded, already with four determinants. The soft↔hard
  gradient is *recoverable* as the second input; it was being sought in the wrong face.
- **"Both sides are driven" is derived at a seam, not assumed** — the strongest single result. The
  vault says pressure foregrounds one pole and leaves the complement *residual*. But a seam's two
  sides are the fold's content (driven) and the remainder now admitted (driven — "what cannot matter
  presses to matter"). A seam just *is* two driven things co-located under one relation.
- **This grounds `tension-zero` architecturally:** the ordinary case has an undriven complement, so
  no cost. *(Report v2 §6's separate charge — that zero is normatively unmotivated — is untouched.)*
- **"Cannot both be had" = prospective foreclosure**, which invokes the operative column and is
  non-third **only by the row-identity** — dependency named. Prospective character at L3 is carried
  by forward-orientation, not modal projection: **the L3 placement survives.**

**What clears is the identification of the quantity and its inputs, not a formula.** The composition
rule is unfixed and is the formal session's. That is the vault's ordinary standard — resilience is
"the measure of truth" with its measure open; remainder is unmeasurable in principle.

## ⚠ The blocker

**The bar tests what fixes the grade. It says nothing about what fixes the category boundary** — and
tension proper is *defined* as the non-acceptance signature on an **objectified** relation. The
**stance axis** is the study's own flagged possibly-new articulation, the Agda does not carry it, and
Frege's criterion demand is aimed at it.

**`03_Phenomenology §Tension` cannot be written without it** — no stance, no category boundary, no
shimmer-orthogonality (which is bar 2's non-redundancy result). **So the clearance does not by itself
unblock integration.**

Two candidate vault homes, both committed, neither tested: **operating-under vs representing-as** (the
ascension mechanism) and **apparatus-opacity vs de-naturalization**. Both are cross-level while the
axis is wanted in-level at L3 — that gap is the work. Route (ii) carries a possibly-false consequence
(you could only be torn about what you have de-naturalized). **Recommended: a short stance-axis bar
before the consolidated session.** PB's call.
