## The reframe

The vault said both "there are only distinctions" and "one fundamental dynamic — distinction-making." Stated as "only distinctions," the primitive is a relation, and value looks either like a second primitive (breaking the monism) or a mere modifier (breaking co-constitution). Integrated statement: **there is only distinction-making — one dynamic with two co-constitutive faces, neither prior: value (driving, mattering) and partition (structural, distinction).**
